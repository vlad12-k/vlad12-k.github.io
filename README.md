### 📊 Data Engineering & Decision Intelligence

**[AirTrafficAnalytics](https://github.com/vlad12-k/air-traffic-analytics)**

An airport-market decision intelligence case study built with Python, SQL and Power BI.

Combines governed data pipelines, data-quality validation, analytical modelling and scenario-based business decision support.

**[Green Energy Dispatch Dashboard](https://github.com/vlad12-k/green-energy-dispatch-dashboard)**

A grid-aware energy operations prototype combining FastAPI, PostgreSQL, React and external carbon-intensity data.

Evaluates synthetic scheduling strategies through an interactive dashboard. The platform remains under development and does not control physical assets.

**[OncoVax IoT Platform](https://github.com/vlad12-k/oncovax-iot-platform)**

An event-driven biotech cold-storage monitoring platform combining simulated MQTT telemetry, alert processing, FastAPI, MongoDB, InfluxDB and Grafana.

Includes automated validation, operational documentation and archived evidence of an earlier hosted deployment.

**[Boutique Hotel Booking System](https://github.com/vlad12-k/boutique-hotel-booking-system)**

A Flask and PostgreSQL operations application under development for a boutique hotel.

Implements booking validation, database integrity, authentication, migrations and operational workflows. Not yet deployed for live guest operations.
